Help

  • Click on the photo to place the next pixel; the index then moves on by step. With step 10 you click every tenth light and the rest are filled in between.
  • Click any lit pixel you can pick out: anchors can be any indices, in any order. Set Next pixel to jump.
  • Drag an anchor to move it, right-click it to delete it.
  • Wheel zoom, right-drag pan (also middle-drag or Space+drag), F fit.
  • [ ] next pixel −/+ step, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo.
  • Pixels before the first anchor or after the last are unmapped; export pins them to the nearest anchor and warns.
  • Work saves itself in this browser. Save project writes a file you can keep beside the photo and reopen later.
